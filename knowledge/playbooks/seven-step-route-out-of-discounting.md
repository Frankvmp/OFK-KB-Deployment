# Seven-Step Route Out of Discounting

*(New here? See AGENTS.md at this repository's root for how to use it.)*

## When to use this

Reach for this once a brand is already trapped in a self-reinforcing discount spiral (the "toilet of discounting") — it's the recovery path back to profit-focused pricing, not a general pricing strategy.

## Purpose

This playbook defines an ordered process for seven-step route out of discounting. The sequence and decision points are retained from the registered evidence.

## Inputs and preconditions

Explicit: being in a self-reinforcing discount-spiral state (Framework, 01:15:28-01:20:21).

## Ordered steps

### 1. Focus on profit, not the sales line

**Step type:** Constraint check — shift the framing to profit before anything else changes.

it begins... by ignoring the sales line, by not worrying about revenues, but worrying instead about the profit within it.

### 2. Get the price right first (research)

**Step type:** Data-gathering — real pricing research, never invented.

*(Adapted for agent execution — states the evidence standard, not a transcript claim.)* The destination price must rest on real evidence of what customers actually value and are willing to pay — genuine customer-sourced pricing evidence, not an internally assumed number dressed up as a price point. If that evidence doesn't exist yet, that gap must be stated honestly before a price is set, not filled with a plausible-sounding figure.

**Invokes:** [Value and profitability](../frameworks/value-and-profitability.md) (Framework).

### 3. Manage senior expectations

**Step type:** Evidence-and-judgement — a real communication act the agent can draft but not perform.

manage senior expectations for the journey ahead and manage them before the journey begins.

### 4. Focus on differentiation

**Step type:** Decision (criteria-guided).

focus on differentiation... there's a very important reason why distinctiveness is important.

**Invokes:** [Positioning and brand codes](../frameworks/positioning-and-brand-codes.md) (Framework).

### 5. Track a real leading indicator during recovery

**Step type:** Data-gathering — real tracked results, never invented; a lead indicator before revenue/profit recovers.

*(Adapted for agent execution — states the evidence standard, not a transcript claim.)* Track real brand-health indicators — Funnel stage movement and Touchpoint experience ratings — as a leading signal during the recovery period, since revenue and profit lag behind. Draw on whatever genuine tracking data already exists or becomes available. If none exists yet, that absence must be stated plainly, not filled with an assumed trend.

**Invokes:** [Brand tracking](../frameworks/brand-tracking.md) (Framework); [Funnel](../frameworks/funnel.md) (Framework); [Touchpoints](../frameworks/touchpoints.md) (Framework).

### 6. Shift spend from discounting to advertising

**Step type:** Decision (criteria-guided) — reallocating real budget from promotion to advertising.

Change the focus in your brand from promotional discount investments to advertising.

### 7. Keep the focus on profit

**Step type:** Constraint check — sustain the profit focus even as revenue recovers; do not revert.

as things begin to improve, keep the focus on profit. Revenues will return, but the focus now, from now on, will be on profit and profit only.

## Output

"There is a way out of the discount toilet." (01:28:29) -- exit from discounting back to profit-focus.
