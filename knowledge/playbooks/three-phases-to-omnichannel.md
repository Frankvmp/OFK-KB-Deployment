# Three Phases to Omnichannel

*(New here? See AGENTS.md at this repository's root for how to use it.)*

## When to use this

Reach for this when assessing or planning a retailer's or brand's channel maturity — diagnosing which phase it's actually in, rather than assuming full integration exists just because multiple channels are live.

## Purpose

This playbook defines an ordered process for three phases to omnichannel. The sequence and decision points are retained from the registered evidence.

## Invokes

- [Omnichannel quality depends on integration, not channel count](../principles/omnichannel-quality-depends-on-integration-not-channel-count.md) (Principle) — governs the move from adding channels to integrating the priority channels well.

## Inputs and preconditions

None stated beyond having 'a particular competence in a particular channel' as the starting point.

## Ordered steps

### 1. Phase 1: single-channel competence

**Step type:** Data-gathering — the real channel history and origin of the retailer, never assumed.

most retailers evolved out of a particular competence in a particular channel.

### 2. Phase 2: embrace other channels without integration

**Step type:** Constraint check — this phase is defined by the ABSENCE of integration; adding channels alone doesn't advance past it.

they begin to embrace the other channel opportunities... they haven't integrated properly.

### 3. Phase 3: full seamless integration

**Step type:** Constraint check — requires consistent, seamless data and brand experience across every channel; anything less stays Phase 2.

That only takes place when a retailer is able to encompass all those different commerce channels in a consistent, seamless way.

### 4. Focus on two or three main channels (McKinsey advice)

**Step type:** Decision (criteria-guided) — select two or three channels to bring to full quality, rather than every channel at once.

there are two or three main channels... the focus for omni-channel success should be on those two or three channels.

### 5. Deliver the omnichannel capability set

**Step type:** Constraint check — the full capability set (buy-online-pickup-in-store, cross-channel transparency, unified loyalty) must be present, not a partial subset.

buying online and picking up in-store or curbside... transparency across channels... loyalty programs...
