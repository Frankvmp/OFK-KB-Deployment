# Settle the Strategy Before Execution

## When to use this

Reach for this once targeting, positioning, and objectives are all drafted, right before tactical execution begins — it's the last checkpoint while the strategy can still change cheaply.

## Purpose

This playbook defines an ordered process for settle the strategy before execution. The sequence and decision points are retained from the registered evidence.

## Inputs and preconditions

Operates on the entire finished strategy (targeting + positioning + objectives together).

## Ordered steps

### 1. Give it time -- let the strategy settle mentally

**Step type:** Constraint check — time-boxed reflection is stated as required ("there'll be no time to change the strategy" once execution begins), not optional.

before strategy ends, give yourself time. Allow your strategy to settle. Think about it in the shower.

### 2. Get external, adversarial review

**Step type:** Evidence-and-judgement — real human review, not something the agent can substitute for.

Buy that really smart, experienced friend of yours a cold beer and take him or her through the strategy... Ask them to kick holes in the strategy.

### 3. Identify flaws and gaps

**Step type:** Evidence-and-judgement.

Is there anything missing? Look for flaws.

### 4. Trim/adjust

**Step type:** Decision (criteria-guided) — removing unnecessary elements once flaws are identified.

Trim off the unnecessary elements... Now is the time to be hard on you and the strategy.

### 5. Check against Lafley's six strategy traps

**Step type:** Constraint check — the strategy must not fall into any of the six named traps.

the great AG Lafley once came up with six strategy traps.

**Invokes:** A.G. Lafley's six strategy traps (Framework). *(not yet drafted)*

A 'locked down' strategy ready for tactical execution.
