# Distribution Cadence (brand-first, phased channel rollout)

## When to use this

*(Process framing — not a sourced claim; no footnote.)* Reach for this when launching or growing distribution for a brand — to sequence channels brand-first (protecting positioning early) rather than defaulting to whichever channel offers the fastest volume.

## Purpose

This playbook defines an ordered process for distribution cadence (brand-first, phased channel rollout). The sequence and decision points are retained from the registered evidence.

## Invokes

- [Early distribution should establish the brand before maximising availability](../principles/early-distribution-should-establish-the-brand-before-maximising-availability.md) (Principle) — governs the brand-first sequence before wider physical availability.
- [Grey-market leakage can become self-reinforcing](../principles/grey-market-leakage-can-become-self-reinforcing.md) (Principle) — distinguishes intended phased expansion from unauthorised parallel distribution.

## Inputs and preconditions

Explicit -- positioning/targeting/objective fit (step 1).

## Ordered steps

### 1. Confirm fit to positioning, targeting, and objective

**Step type:** Constraint check — the channel choice must fit all three strategic answers, not just be the most convenient option.

it was the right distribution choice, partly... a perfect fit between the positioning of Fiji Water... a fit to the targeting... the objective at the heart of the Fiji Water launch. [^origin]

**Invokes:** [Target → position → objective](../frameworks/target-position-objective.md) (Framework).

### 2. Launch on-premise / brand-building channel first

**Step type:** Constraint check — the brand-building channel must come first; a scale-first launch is the stated failure mode.

what we're creating is what we call an on-premise experience. [^step-2]

**Invokes:** On-premise and off-premise distribution (Framework). *(not yet drafted)*

### 3. Let cadence pull the brand into adjacent, then mainstream retail

**Step type:** Evidence-and-judgement — real-world channel cadence the agent can advise on and track, not perform.

The cadence from the top restaurants into the medium-level restaurants also carries on into what would then become our retail and grocery sales... that filtered down to the very big discount national retail chains. [^step-3]

### 4. Recognize the three distinct roles distribution plays over time

**Step type:** Evidence-and-judgement — judging which of the three roles (brand, profit, revenue) the current phase calls for.

distribution plays three distinct roles. It can be, especially in initial phase, all about brand... Over time... more scale and more profit... finally... the scalable, very profitable, incredibly big revenues. [^step-4]

### 5. Do it gradually, not all at once

**Step type:** Constraint check — gradual, selective rollout is required; switching on all distribution at once is the stated failure mode.

when you grow a brand's distribution over time, you need to think about it in different phases... to get everything, we may have to do it in a gradual and selective fashion. [^step-5]

## Output

Balanced brand + profit + revenue ("Three Gifts of Distribution", Framework) without sacrificing brand equity or price.
