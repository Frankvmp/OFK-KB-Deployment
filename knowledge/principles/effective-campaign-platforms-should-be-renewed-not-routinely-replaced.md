# Effective campaign platforms should be renewed, not routinely replaced

*(New here? See AGENTS.md at this repository's root for how to use it.)*

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when an effective campaign is being replaced mainly because it is old, familiar to the internal team, or assumed to have worn out.

## Principle

Campaign age alone is not evidence of declining effectiveness. The cited studies found little general wearout and found that most replacement campaigns introduced for wearout reasons performed worse than the work they replaced.

Plan for campaignability: invest in a strong central idea that can persist while individual executions are renewed and refreshed. A longer useful life can shift later budgets from repeated creative replacement toward media delivery.

## Why it matters

Routine replacement discards accumulated memory and can substitute novelty for evidence. A renewable platform can amortise creative investment and preserve consistency without repeating one execution unchanged.

## Related distinctions

Persistence does not require keeping a campaign after the product, strategy, or evidence has made it irrelevant. Renewal changes executions while retaining the effective central platform.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not retain a campaign solely because old campaigns can remain effective.
- Test current performance rather than assuming either wearout or permanence.
- Replace work when the strategy, product, or evidence requires it.
- Refresh executions without casually abandoning the central idea and codes.
