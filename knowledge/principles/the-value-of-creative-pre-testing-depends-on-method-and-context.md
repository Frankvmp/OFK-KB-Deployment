# The value of creative pre-testing depends on method and context

*(New here? See AGENTS.md at this repository's root for how to use it.)*

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when creative pre-testing is treated as either automatically necessary or inherently destructive to strong advertising.

## Principle

Creative pre-testing involves a real trade-off. Artificial presentation can remove the context, executional craft, and limited attention of actual advertising exposure. Newer methods can test near-final animatics quickly, measure emotional response, support refinements, and help manage stakeholders. Its value therefore depends on the method's realism, cost, predictive quality, and decision context.

## Why it matters

A universal rule for or against testing ignores both weak testing designs and useful evidence. The decision should examine what the test can validly reveal and how its result will be used.

## Related distinctions

Pre-testing can inform creative judgement; it does not mechanically produce the big idea or remove the need for trusted creative expertise.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not generalise from traditional focus-group testing to every modern method.
- Do not treat predictive evidence as certainty.
- Do not let stakeholder reassurance become the sole definition of creative quality.
- Do not reject useful evidence merely to preserve an untested execution.
