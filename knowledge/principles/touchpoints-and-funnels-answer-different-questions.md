# Touchpoints and funnels answer different questions

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when a customer-experience map and a market funnel are being treated as interchangeable.

## Principle

A funnel represents sequential movement through the wider market and supports strategic objective setting. A touchpoint map follows an individual's idiosyncratic and continuing experience with the brand and supports product or experience improvement.

## Why it matters

Combining the two views can make individual experiences look like universal market stages or make market conversion data stand in for the lived customer journey.

## Related distinctions

The tools can inform one another, but they retain different units of analysis, shapes, and decision purposes. One should not be relabelled as the other.

## Invokes

- [Touchpoints](../frameworks/touchpoints.md) (Framework) — maps and evaluates the individual's continuing experience.
- [Funnel](../frameworks/funnel.md) (Framework) — diagnoses sequential market movement for objective setting.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not use touchpoint ratings as funnel conversion measures.
- Do not treat every individual's journey as the same sequence.
- Do not use funnel stages as a complete map of product experience.
