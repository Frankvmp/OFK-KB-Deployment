# Align marketing and sales targeting choices

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when marketing and sales select accounts or segments independently.

## Principle

Marketing and sales should share the same segmentation, compare whom each function intends to target, and agree how their different activities feed one another.

## Why it matters

Separate target choices can duplicate effort, leave important segments unattended, or send conflicting signals about strategic priorities.

## Related distinctions

Alignment does not require marketing and sales to perform identical work. It requires their target choices and handoffs to form one coherent commercial approach.

## Invokes

- [Module 4 Targeting Sequence](../playbooks/module-4-targeting-sequence-strategic-framing-philosophy-choice-apply-criteria-build-portrait-ensure-clarity.md) (Playbook) — provides the shared target and non-target decisions.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not let either function infer the other's target list.
- Do not treat a shared segmentation as proof that target choices are aligned.
- Record how marketing interest or leads pass into sales activity.
