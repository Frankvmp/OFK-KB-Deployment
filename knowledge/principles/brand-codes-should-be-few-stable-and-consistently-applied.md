# Brand codes should be few, stable, and consistently applied

*(New here? See AGENTS.md at this repository's root for how to use it.)*

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when a brand has too many identifiers, changes them because internal teams are bored, or applies them only in selected communications.

## Principle

A brand should select a small palette of codes. “Logo plus three” is a rule of thumb for keeping the set manageable, not a universal fixed requirement.

Once selected, the codes should be held stable and applied across the touchpoints customers encounter. Repetition over time builds recognisability; adding or changing codes can weaken it.

## Why it matters

Distinctiveness depends on consistent exposure. A large or frequently changing palette divides that exposure among competing identifiers.

## Related distinctions

Codes may be arbitrary identifiers. They do not all need to explain the position, but they must reliably identify the brand.

## Invokes

- [Positioning and brand codes](../frameworks/positioning-and-brand-codes.md) (Framework) — provides the code-assessment method and relationship to positioning.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Treat “logo plus three” as guidance, not a mandatory count.
- Do not add codes merely to create variety.
- Do not change established codes because internal teams tire of them.
- Apply the selected palette beyond advertising wherever customers encounter the brand.
