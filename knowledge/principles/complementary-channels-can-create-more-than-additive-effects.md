# Complementary channels can create more-than-additive effects

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when a media decision is framed as choosing one winning channel or concentrating all investment in a single medium.

## Principle

A deliberate channel mix can outperform either channel used alone. Combining online and offline media, or channels with different strengths, can create a positive synergistic effect rather than merely dividing the same result between tools. [^mix]

## Why it matters

Different channels can expose, repeat, and extend a campaign. Complementary reach and roles can make the combined system stronger than one isolated delivery path.

## Related distinctions

Synergy is not guaranteed by adding channels. The channels must be suitable, complementary, coherently integrated, and adequately funded.

## Invokes

- [Long-term brand building and short-term activation](../frameworks/long-term-brand-building-and-short-term-activation.md) (Framework) — contains the integrated-communications sequence for diagnosing and combining media tools.
- [Strategy directs each tactical P](../frameworks/strategy-directs-each-tactical-p.md) (Framework) — keeps the channel mix aligned with the governing strategy.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not choose a mix only to maximise the number of channels.
- Do not assume two weak or mismatched channels will create synergy.
- Preserve a common strategy and recognisable campaign across the mix.
- Check that each channel has a defined role.
