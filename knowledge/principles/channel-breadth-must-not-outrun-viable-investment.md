# Channel breadth must not outrun viable investment

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when a media plan adds channels to increase reach or effectiveness without checking whether each channel can receive enough investment to work.

## Principle

Campaign effectiveness can increase as channels are added. In the cited Analytic Partners analysis, this improvement remained after controlling for budget. The broader pattern is conditional: the available budget must be large enough to fund the selected channels at useful points on their response curves. [^breadth]

## Why it matters

Channel diversity can create additional effects, but spreading a limited budget too thinly can leave several channels below viable investment.

## Related distinctions

The evidence does not establish one universal optimum number of channels. It supports adding suitable channels when they can be adequately funded.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not convert “more is more” into an unlimited-channel rule.
- Do not count channels without checking investment adequacy.
- Do not assume every additional channel is suitable for the target, position, objective, or message.
- Do not infer a universal channel count from campaign averages.
