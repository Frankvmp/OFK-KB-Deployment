# Segmentation is an option, not a default

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle before assuming that every market must be divided into target segments.

## Principle

Segmentation should be evaluated as an option rather than treated as the automatic form of marketing. If meaningful and actionable differences do not emerge, mass marketing may be the appropriate route. [^no-useful-segments]

The decision should compare the value of recognising differences with the scale and growth advantages of serving commonalities. [^strategic-options]

## Why it matters

Automatic segmentation can create distinctions that do not improve action. Automatic mass marketing can hide differences that matter. Treating both as strategic options keeps the choice tied to evidence.

## Related distinctions

Defining the mass market is required in either case. That definition establishes who is in scope and provides the base from which either a whole-market approach or a segmented approach can proceed.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not segment solely because segmentation is conventional practice.
- Do not choose mass marketing solely to avoid analytical effort.
- Do not skip the mass-market definition, whichever strategic option is later selected.
