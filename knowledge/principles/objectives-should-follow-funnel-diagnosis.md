# Objectives should follow funnel diagnosis

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when objectives are being set from a sales target, revenue trend, or isolated outcome measure without a diagnostic view of the customer journey.

## Principle

Objective setting should begin with a populated funnel. The funnel shows the stages through which the target market moves and provides the evidence needed to choose where marketing should intervene. [^funnel-first]

Sales results remain commercially important, but they reveal only the outcome. They do not explain where customers were lost or which earlier stage created the result. [^sales-limit]

## Why it matters

An isolated outcome can prompt plausible but misdirected action. Funnel diagnosis connects the objective to a specific market stage, making the later choice of tactics more defensible.

## Related distinctions

A funnel diagnoses where movement is being lost. It does not by itself explain why the loss occurs; that requires further customer or market evidence.

## Invokes

- [Diagnosis → Strategy → Tactics](../frameworks/diagnosis-strategy-tactics.md) (Framework) — places funnel-informed objective selection between diagnosis and tactical execution.
- [Funnel](../frameworks/funnel.md) (Framework) — provides the diagnostic view from the target market to recurring revenue.
- [Build and Populate a Custom Marketing Funnel](../playbooks/build-and-populate-a-custom-marketing-funnel.md) (Playbook) — creates the evidence base from which objectives are selected.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not treat revenue, sales, or market share alone as a marketing objective.
- Do not choose a funnel stage before the funnel has been populated with evidence.
- Do not infer the cause of a stage gap from its size alone.
