# Funnels must fit the market and buying process

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when a standard funnel is being copied into a plan without checking how customers actually move through the category.

## Principle

No single funnel describes every purchase process. A useful funnel must reflect the actual market, offer, and customer decision journey rather than importing a textbook sequence wholesale. [^no-universal-funnel]

Its main stages should be derived from customer evidence, kept few and hierarchical, and extended beyond purchase only when repurchase, advocacy, or churn materially belongs in the process. [^custom-stages]

## Why it matters

A generic funnel can hide category-specific barriers and create objectives for stages that do not represent the real decision. Custom design makes the diagnostic structure relevant to the business.

## Related distinctions

Custom does not mean unconstrained. The stages still need a logical sequence, a defined target-market base, and measures that can be populated with evidence.

## Invokes

- [Funnel](../frameworks/funnel.md) (Framework) — defines the diagnostic structure and its interpretation.
- [Build and Populate a Custom Marketing Funnel](../playbooks/build-and-populate-a-custom-marketing-funnel.md) (Playbook) — derives and measures the market-specific stages.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not assume awareness, consideration, preference, and purchase are universal stages.
- Do not add stages merely to make the funnel appear comprehensive.
- Do not confuse a market-specific funnel with an unrestricted customer-journey map.
