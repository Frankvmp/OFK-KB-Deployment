# Market intelligence must circulate, and response is judgement

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when customer or competitor research stays inside marketing, when another function cannot access the intelligence, or when a team treats listening to customers as a promise to deliver every requested feature.

## Principle

Market orientation is an organisation-wide discipline. Customer and competitor intelligence must be generated, circulated beyond the marketing department, and brought into the organisation's response system. [^organisational-orientation]

A response to market intelligence is a judgement about how the organisation should act. Responsiveness does not always mean doing what a customer asks; it can include saying no or doing nothing while still taking the insight into account. [^response-judgement]

## Why it matters

A marketing team cannot represent the market effectively if the rest of the organisation remains disconnected from the intelligence it generates. This is described as “irrigating” information — pumping it around the company so there is a company-wide focus on customer needs. [^organisational-orientation]

This Principle protects the difference between listening and automatic compliance. It allows an organisation to acknowledge an insight, assess it, and choose an appropriate response rather than treating every request as an instruction. [^response-judgement]

## Related distinctions

This Principle is narrower than [Market orientation](../frameworks/market-orientation.md): it highlights the circulation-and-response implications of the Framework's organisational definition rather than restating the whole Framework.

It is also distinct from [Research informs understanding; it does not supply strategy](../frameworks/research-informs-understanding-not-strategy.md). Research may generate understanding, but this Principle concerns what happens to intelligence across the organisation and how the organisation responds.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not confuse responsiveness with agreeing to every customer request. [^response-judgement]
- Do not treat intelligence as useful merely because marketing has collected it; the source explicitly places value on sharing it across the organisation. [^organisational-orientation]
