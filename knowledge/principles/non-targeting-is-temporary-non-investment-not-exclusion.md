# Non-targeting is temporary non-investment, not exclusion

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when stakeholders interpret a non-target decision as closing the business to those customers forever.

## Principle

A non-target is a group that will not receive focused marketing investment during the current planning period. It does not have to be refused when it approaches the organisation. [^non-exclusion]

Targeting choices normally apply to a defined horizon, such as the next financial year, and can be revisited when the next planning cycle begins. [^annual-horizon]

## Why it matters

This makes sacrifice operational without turning it into unnecessary exclusion. It also reduces pressure to target everyone merely because future opportunities remain possible.

## Related distinctions

Accepting business from a non-target is different from spending scarce marketing resources to pursue it. A later review is different from weakening the current decision.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not allocate targeted resources to a group while calling it a non-target.
- Do not promise that a non-target will remain excluded indefinitely.
- State the review horizon alongside the target decision.
