# Segment B2B markets at the account level

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when designing B2B segmentation and deciding whether firms and individual buying-committee members belong on the same map.

## Principle

In B2B markets, segment qualifying organisations at the firm or account level. Keep the later description of buyers, users, and other decision makers inside selected accounts as a separate layer.

## Why it matters

Mixing organisations and individuals in one segmentation rapidly multiplies combinations and obscures the unit being selected. An account-level map preserves a stable market unit while still allowing detailed buying-committee portraits after targeting.

## Related distinctions

Market definition may identify both qualifying accounts and the people involved in purchase decisions. That two-level definition does not mean both levels should become segmentation units.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not place firms and individual decision makers as equivalent members of one segmentation.
- Do not ignore the buying committee; defer its detailed portrait until target accounts are selected.
- Apply this rule to B2B account structures, not automatically to consumer markets.
