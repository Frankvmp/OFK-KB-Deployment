# Product success requires quality and market preparation

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when product quality and marketing support are presented as substitutes, or when development work has displaced launch preparation.

## Principle

Product quality is a necessary condition of commercial success. Communications or prominent advocates cannot reliably compensate for an offering that fails to work well. [^quality]

Quality alone is not sufficient. New products can fail when organisations focus so heavily on development that they neglect market preparation and communication of the product's value. [^communication]

Product development therefore requires both product orientation and market orientation. Concentrated development work should not remove attention from the market conditions surrounding the launch. [^orientation-balance]

## Why it matters

A product-led organisation can build something strong that customers never understand or discover. A communications-led organisation can create demand that the product cannot satisfy.

## Related distinctions

Market preparation does not repair a defective product. Product quality does not remove the need to establish target, position, objectives, pricing, communication, and distribution.

## Invokes

- [Four Ps](../frameworks/four-ps.md) (Framework) — coordinates product with the other tactical requirements.
- [Market orientation](../frameworks/market-orientation.md) (Framework) — maintains attention to customers and the market during development.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not treat promotion as a substitute for product quality.
- Do not postpone market preparation until development is complete.
- Do not assume passing product tests guarantees market adoption.
