# Segment names should carry insight, not priority

*(New here? See AGENTS.md at this repository's root for how to use it.)*

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when assigning labels to completed segments.

## Principle

A segment name should help people remember the group's defining behaviour, issue, or insight.

It should not encode a targeting decision, rely on an arbitrary naming theme, or expose the organisation to derogatory language.

## Why it matters

Names become the portable shorthand through which the organisation remembers and discusses the map. A poor name can hide the evidence, pre-empt strategy, or create reputational harm.

## Related distinctions

Naming describes a segment; it does not rank it. Labels such as “Gold” or “Priority One” belong to a later targeting judgement, not to market diagnosis.

## Invokes

- [Meaningful Actionable Grid](../playbooks/meaningful-actionable-grid-10-stage-segmentation-construction-playbook.md) (Playbook) — includes segment naming after population, value, and share are established.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not use names that state target priority.
- Do not use themed labels whose connection to the segment breaks down.
- Do not use labels that customers or colleagues could reasonably regard as derogatory.
