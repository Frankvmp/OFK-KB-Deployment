# Customer alternatives define segment-relative competition

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when a business defines competitors only by industry category, assumes the same competitors matter to every customer, or makes a positioning decision without checking the alternatives customers actually consider.

## Principle

A competitor is a customer-relevant alternative, not only a firm that the organisation has placed in its own category. Customers may consider alternatives from outside that category, and the alternatives identified by the company may not be part of the customer's decision set.

Competition can change with the customer segment. One segment may consider one alternative while another segment considers a different one, so the relevant competitive set is not fixed across the whole market.

## Why it matters

The Principle changes how an agent frames competitor analysis. It should ask what alternatives customers consider in the relevant segment instead of importing a fixed competitor list from the organisation's internal category definition.

This supports the transition from market orientation into [Segmentation process](../playbooks/segmentation-process-top-level-5-step-sequence.md), [Targeting sequence](../playbooks/module-4-targeting-sequence-strategic-framing-philosophy-choice-apply-criteria-build-portrait-ensure-clarity.md), and [Positioning and brand codes](../frameworks/positioning-and-brand-codes.md), where customer perception and segment choice affect the marketing decision.

## Related distinctions

This Principle does not provide a competitor-analysis procedure or a fixed competitor list. It states the reasoning rule that should govern those later decisions.

It is also not a claim that all alternatives are equally relevant. Relevance must be established from the customer's decision-making set in the segment under consideration.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not define the competitive set only from the organisation's category label.
- Do not assume one fixed competitor set applies equally to every segment.
