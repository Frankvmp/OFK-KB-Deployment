# The funnel base must match the selected target

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when defining the population at the top of a funnel or comparing funnels for different segments.

## Principle

The funnel's 100 percent base must represent the population the strategy actually targets. A whole-market strategy begins with the whole potential market; a segment strategy begins with the complete selected segment. [^target-base]

Every later stage should remain expressed against that original base. This preserves a consistent view of how much of the target population reaches each stage. [^base-convention]

## Why it matters

A base that is too narrow can conceal the largest opportunity and direct the objective at the wrong stage. Changing denominators between stages also makes the funnel harder to interpret.

## Related distinctions

A stage-to-stage conversion rate uses the preceding stage as its denominator. The displayed funnel population can still remain anchored to the original 100 percent base.

- [Build and Populate a Custom Marketing Funnel](../playbooks/build-and-populate-a-custom-marketing-funnel.md) (Playbook) — establishes the base before deriving and populating later stages.
- [Segmentation Process](../playbooks/segmentation-process-top-level-5-step-sequence.md) (Playbook) — supplies the total market and any selected segment used as the base.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not use only already-recognised or already-engaged customers as the base when the target is broader.
- Do not combine segment funnels whose populations follow materially different paths.
- Do not confuse funnel-stage percentages with stage-to-stage conversion rates.
