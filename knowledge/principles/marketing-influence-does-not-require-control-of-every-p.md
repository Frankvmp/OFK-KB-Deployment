# Marketing influence does not require control of every P

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when marketing responsibility is being limited to communications because other functions formally own product, pricing, or distribution.

## Principle

Marketing should contribute to decisions across product, pricing, communications, and distribution. A communications-only remit is a restricted application of marketing. [^restricted-view]

Direct control is not the relevant test. Marketers may share decisions with other functions or agencies while still providing meaningful influence and input across all four tactical areas. [^input-not-control]

## Why it matters

Treating organisational ownership as the boundary of marketing removes the customer and strategy perspective from decisions that shape the market experience.

## Related distinctions

Influence is not unilateral authority. This Principle requires informed participation, not that the marketing function overrules the teams responsible for delivery.

## Invokes

- [Four Ps](../frameworks/four-ps.md) (Framework) — identifies the four tactical areas into which marketing should contribute.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not equate lack of formal control with lack of responsibility for input.
- Do not reduce marketing to promotion or agency management.
- Do not use marketing influence to bypass operational expertise or decision rights.
