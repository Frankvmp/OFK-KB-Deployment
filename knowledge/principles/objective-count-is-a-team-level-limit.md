# Objective count is a team-level limit

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when objectives are being allocated separately to every product, segment, or workstream without checking the total load on the responsible team.

## Principle

A team should carry only a small handful of strategic objectives. Too many weaken focus and recall, while a single objective may be insufficient when several coordinated initiatives are necessary.

The limit applies across the team's products and segments, not independently to each one. Local objective lists accumulate into the real set the same people must understand and deliver.

## Why it matters

Objective proliferation recreates the absence of strategy: everything receives attention and resources are spread too thinly. A team-level limit exposes the choices hidden by separate product plans.

## Related distinctions

A small handful is a planning guardrail, not a universal mathematical optimum. The source offers three to five as guidance while emphasising that context still matters.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not multiply the objective allowance by every product or segment.
- Do not reduce the list to one when the strategy genuinely requires coordinated initiatives.
- Do not retain an objective merely because a stakeholder owns it.
