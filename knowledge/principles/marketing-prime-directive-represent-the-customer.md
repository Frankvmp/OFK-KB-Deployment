# Marketing's prime directive is to represent the customer

## When to use this

Reach for this Principle when an organisation has many functions with legitimate internal priorities but no clear role responsible for bringing the customer's perspective into decisions and keeping the wider organisation connected to the market.

## Principle

Marketing's distinctive role is to speak for, understand, and bring the customer's perspective into the organisation. The marketing team should be market oriented, while the wider organisation should remain in tune with the market through the circulation of market intelligence. [^prime-directive]

## Why it matters

The Principle gives an agent a role-based test for broad marketing questions: marketing is not only a communications function or a sales-support function. It has a responsibility to represent the customer and ensure that the customer perspective has influence inside the organisation. [^prime-directive] [^prime-directive-recap]

The source presents this as a responsibility that no other organisational function will necessarily perform for the organisation. If the customer voice is not brought in, shared, and allowed to influence the company, the organisation is weakened. [^prime-directive-recap]

## Related distinctions

This Principle is not a claim that marketing is the only function with customer-facing responsibilities. The source says that all organisational functions have essential roles, while assigning this customer-representation responsibility specifically to marketing. [^prime-directive-recap]

It is related to [Market intelligence must circulate, and response is judgement](market-intelligence-must-circulate-and-response-is-judgement.md), which focuses on the organisation-wide movement and use of intelligence. This Principle focuses on marketing's distinctive responsibility within that system.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- Do not reduce marketing's role to advertising or sales support. [^prime-directive]
- Do not use this Principle to claim that marketing should comply automatically with every customer request; the related responsiveness Principle preserves the judgement boundary. [^prime-directive-recap]
